## 📊 ATS Score Calculator

### What is ATS?
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by recruiters to filter resumes. This calculator evaluates how ATS-friendly your resume is.

### Scoring Factors (100 points total)

1. **Contact Information (10 points)**
   - Name, email, phone number
   - Complete contact info improves score

2. **Skills Section (20 points)**
   - Optimal: 5-15 skills
   - Too few or too many reduces score

3. **Work Experience (25 points)**
   - Optimal: 2-5 experiences
   - Shows career progression

4. **Education (15 points)**
   - Having education listed improves score

5. **Keywords & Formatting (15 points)**
   - Industry-relevant keywords
   - Optimal: 5-10 keywords

6. **Resume Length & Structure (10 points)**
   - Optimal: 400-800 words
   - Too short or too long reduces score

7. **Red Flags (penalties up to -5 points)**
   - Outdated sections (Objective, References)
   - Unnecessary information

### Score Grades
- **A+ (90-100)**: Excellent - Highly ATS-optimized
- **A (80-89)**: Very Good - Well-optimized
- **B (70-79)**: Good - Decent optimization
- **C (60-69)**: Fair - Needs improvement
- **D (50-59)**: Needs Improvement - Significant gaps
- **F (0-49)**: Poor - Major optimization needed

### Features
- ✅ **Automatic Calculation** - ATS score calculated when resume is uploaded
- ✅ **Detailed Breakdown** - See scores for each factor
- ✅ **Visual Indicators** - Color-coded progress bars
- ✅ **Recommendations** - Actionable tips to improve score
- ✅ **Priority Levels** - High, medium, low priority recommendations

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Upload a Resume**
   - ATS score is automatically calculated
   - View score in the resume list (badge next to name)
   - Click on a resume to see detailed ATS analysis

2. **View ATS Score**
   - Select a resume from the list
   - Detailed ATS score card appears below
   - See breakdown by category
   - Read recommendations to improve

3. **Improve Your Resume**
   - Follow the recommendations
   - Focus on high-priority items first
   - Re-upload to see improved score

## 🎯 Tips for High ATS Scores

1. **Include Complete Contact Info** - Name, email, phone
2. **List 5-15 Relevant Skills** - Not too few, not too many
3. **Show Work Experience** - 2-5 positions is optimal
4. **Use Industry Keywords** - Match job description keywords
5. **Keep Length Optimal** - 400-800 words
6. **Remove Outdated Sections** - No objective, no references line
7. **Use Standard Formatting** - Simple, clean layout
